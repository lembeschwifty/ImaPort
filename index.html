ImaPort

Portable image file converter. One self-contained HTML file: save it anywhere and open it in any browser, on any platform, even fully offline. Convert PNG, JPG, WEBP, GIF, BMP, SVG, HEIC and more to PNG, JPG or WEBP, with optional resizing. Images never leave your device.

Supported inputs: anything your browser can decode (PNG, JPG, WEBP, GIF, BMP, SVG, AVIF where supported), plus HEIC/HEIF via an optional decoder that downloads on first use. Outputs: PNG (lossless), JPG and WEBP (quality control), with optional resize (presets, percentage, or exact dimensions). GIF input uses its first frame; animated output is not supported.
